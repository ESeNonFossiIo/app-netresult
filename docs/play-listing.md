## English (en-US)

**App name:** Net Result: Tennis Tracker

**Short description:** Log tennis matches, score live on phone or watch, and track your win rate.

**Full description:**

Net Result is a simple, fast tennis match tracker. Log every match you play, watch your win rate over time, see
how you do against each opponent, and score the match live from your phone or your watch.

No account, no ads, no internet connection needed. Your matches stay on your phone.

LOG A MATCH IN SECONDS
• Pick the date, type or tap your opponent, enter each set score and add a note
• Win, draw or loss is worked out for you
• Played a match tie-break instead of a third set? Mark it with TB and it still counts as a set
• Save a draft of a half-finished entry and pick it up later

SCORE LIVE, ON YOUR PHONE OR YOUR WATCH
• Tap who won each point; the app knows tennis: 15, 30, 40, deuce, advantage, tie-break at 6-6, best of three sets
• Undo fixes a mis-tap
• The screen stays on while you play
• Finish early and the score so far is kept

WEAR OS APP
• Score the match from your wrist; the phone and watch stay in step, Undo included
• Start a match on either device and the other offers to join it
• Records a tennis workout with your heart rate and calories
• Warm-up mode records vitals before the match and continues into it
• If the phone is out of range, the watch keeps the match and sends it later

HEALTH CONNECT (OPTIONAL)
• Allow it once and your matches and warm-ups are saved as tennis workouts in Health Connect, where Google Fit and
  other fitness apps can show them
• Net Result only writes workouts; it never reads your existing health data

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
• Matches won, drawn and lost, sets won and lost, set win percentage and overall win rate
• Filter your history by opponent, or group matches by opponent
• Tap any match to fix scores, opponent, date or notes

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no tracking, no ads, nothing uploaded
• Export to CSV or JSON, import on a new phone; importing the same file twice is safe
• Free and open source

MAKE IT YOURS
• Four colour themes: Ball, Clay, Grass and Hard court
• Follows your phone's light and dark mode
• English, Italiano, Português, Español and Ελληνικά

Source code and bug reports: https://github.com/ESeNonFossiIo/app-netresult
