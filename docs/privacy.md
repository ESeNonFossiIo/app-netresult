# Net Result privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-30_

Net Result is a free, open-source tennis match tracker for Android and Wear OS. This policy explains what the
app does with your information. The short version: **it stays on your devices, and we never receive it.**

## Who is responsible

Net Result is published by Mauro Bardelloni ("I", "me"). Contact: esenonfossiio@gmail.com, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/ESeNonFossiIo/app-netresult/issues).

## What the app stores

- **Matches you log:** date, opponent name, set scores, notes, and drafts.
- **Live match state:** the score of a match in progress, so the phone and watch can stay in step.
- **Settings:** theme and language.
- **On the watch, while you play:** heart rate, calories burned and the start and end time of the workout.

All of this is stored privately on your phone (and, for a match in progress or not yet sent, on your watch).
The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use the internet.

## What leaves your devices

Nothing is sent to me or to any server. Only two transfers exist, both optional or local:

1. **Phone and watch:** the score and workout data travel directly between your own paired phone and watch over
   Bluetooth, using the Google Wear OS Data Layer. Messages go to the other copy of Net Result only.
2. **Health Connect (only if you allow it):** when you finish a match or warm-up, the app writes a tennis
   exercise session, heart-rate samples and active calories to Android's Health Connect on your phone. Other apps
   that you have allowed to read Health Connect, such as Google Fit, can then see them. You choose this in
   Settings > Health Connect, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Health Connect. Net Result only
   writes to Health Connect. It does not read your existing health data.

Heart-rate and calorie data are health data. They are used only to show you your workout and to write it to
Health Connect if you allow it. They are not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else.

## Permissions

- **Health Connect: write exercise, heart rate, active calories** (phone): to save your workout, only if you allow it.
- **Body sensors / Heart rate, Physical activity** (watch): to measure your heart rate and calories during a
  match or warm-up.
- **Wake lock** (watch): to keep the screen available while you score a point.

The app never asks for access to your files, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Backups use Android's own
file picker.

## Your control

- **Export** your matches to CSV or JSON, and **import** them again, in Settings.
- **Reset all data** in Settings wipes the matches stored on the phone.
- **Uninstalling** the app deletes its data. Workouts already written to Health Connect stay there until you
  delete them in Health Connect.
- **Withdraw** Health Connect access at any time in Health Connect or Android settings.

## Children

Net Result is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
