Welcome to Net Result — our first release!

Keep a record of every tennis match you play:
• Log games with date, opponent, set-by-set score and notes
• Win / Loss / Draw set automatically from the score
• Browse your history or filter by opponent
• Stats and win rate, overall and per opponent
• Export and import as CSV or JSON (duplicates skipped)
• In English, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish and Greek
• Fully offline — your data stays on your device
