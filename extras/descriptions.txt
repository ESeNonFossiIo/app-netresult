NET RESULT — STORE LISTING DESCRIPTIONS
========================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (79/80 chars)
--------------------------------
Track your tennis matches: log scores, filter by opponent, watch your win rate.


FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
Net Result is a simple, fast tennis match tracker. Log every match you play, set by set, and watch your win rate grow over time — no account, no ads, no internet connection required.

LOG MATCHES IN SECONDS
Record the date, your opponent, the score of each set, and optional notes. The win or loss is worked out automatically from the set scores you enter — you never have to tag the result yourself.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
The home screen greets you with your overall win rate and total matches played. Dive into the history screen for the full picture: browse every match you've logged, filter by opponent to see how a specific rivalry is going, and watch the summary stats update to match your filter.

EDIT ANYTIME
Made a typo? Every match can be edited or deleted straight from the history list — scores, opponent, date, and notes.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
All matches are stored locally on your device in a private database. Export your full history to CSV or JSON at any time, and import it back on a new phone. A reset option lets you start fresh whenever you want.

SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE
Available in English, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Greek.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Net Result is open source under the MIT license. No tracking, no ads, no sign-up — just you, your matches, and your net result.

Whether you play league matches every weekend or the occasional friendly, Net Result gives you a clear record of how your game is trending. Log it, filter it, and settle once and for all who really wins more often.
